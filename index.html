    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    How long does cooking take?

    Your feast is ready in 15 minutes. Pan grill for 2 to 5 minutes each side until golden, or air fry at 200°C for 8 to 12 minutes each side on a lined tray.

    Do I need to marinate anything?

    No. Every breast arrives already marinated with real spices. Open the pack and cook.

    Are there preservatives?

    No added preservatives. The flavour comes from real spices and ingredients.

    How long does it keep?

    Use within 12 days of manufacture. Keep at -2 to 4°C and use within 2 days of opening. Unopened packs can be stored in the freezer for up to 30 days.

    Do I need to defrost it?

    No. Bring it to room temperature before cooking. There's no defrosting step.

    Is it halal?

    Yes. Every pack carries the halal mark.

    Can I mix flavours?

    Yes. Build a custom 6-pack box from any of the seven flavours.
